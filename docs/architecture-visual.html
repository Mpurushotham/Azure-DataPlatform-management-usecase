The platform, top to bottom

Six layers. The four upper bands are the data path and read downward; orchestration drives processing from below, and the foundation is cross-cutting.

Nothing holds a key

The trust chain from a person or a pod through to bytes on disk. Every hop is a federated or managed identity; no step presents a stored secret.

What is not shown

Private endpoints, NAT gateway, customer-managed keys, zone-redundant storage and Defender plans exist in the prod Terraform root and are not deployed here — a Free Trial subscription with a €50 budget cannot fund them. Unity Catalog grants are written but not applied, pending SCIM provisioning of the Entra groups. Each gap is recorded in docs/COVERAGE.md rather than implied away.
